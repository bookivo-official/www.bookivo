BOOKIVO launch update — 23 November 2026

Included
- Checkout no longer sends or requires a guest email OTP. Account sign-in still verifies ownership before opening a private bookshelf.
- Guest order access is tied to a random proof for that specific order, never just the typed email.
- New payable totals are rounded down to whole rupees in both the browser and database. Example: 49.75 becomes 49.
- Cashfree checkout uses the backend-returned environment. Production requests use api.cashfree.com and the production checkout SDK mode.
- Mobile product galleries are 16:9, descriptions precede purchase buttons, and support buttons sit at the bottom.
- Desktop purchase buttons remain initially visible; the scrolling Buy Now bar is compact and centred.
- Announcement stays at the top. Mobile sign-in has no PRIME launcher or empty vertical scrolling.
- Existing signup emails switch to Login with a red notification.
